Step-by-Step Workflow

From confirmed career profile to approved application files

CareerCraft AI enforces a clear verification loop: Extract, Confirm, Analyse, Match, Plan, Generate, Validate, Review, Approve, and Export.

  1. 01

    Upload your existing CV

    Upload a PDF or Word document into private storage, or skip uploading and enter your background manually during onboarding.

  2. 02

    Build and confirm your career profile

    Review extracted records alongside your existing profile. You decide which items to keep, edit, or merge—nothing is overwritten silently.

  3. 03

    Paste a job description

    Provide the full description, application instructions, and screening questions from any role you have independently identified.

  4. 04

    Analyse requirements & compatibility

    Review mandatory vs. preferred qualifications and inspect which confirmed skills match—and which gaps require clarification.

  5. 05

    Generate tailored drafts

    Run Quick Generate or step-by-step Guided Generate to produce draft CVs, cover letters, and question responses validated against your profile.

  6. 06

    Review, edit, approve & export

    Edit content in the split workspace, choose your CV template, explicitly approve the final document, and download real PDF or DOCX files.
