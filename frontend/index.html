Workflow

How CareerCraft AI works

A transparent, six-step preparation sequence where you review and confirm every piece of information before exporting.

  1. 01

    Upload your existing CV

    Import a PDF or Word (.docx) CV into private storage, or start entering your background directly.

  2. 02

    Build and confirm your career profile

    Review extracted roles, education, skills, projects, and certifications. Nothing enters your profile without your confirmation.

  3. 03

    Paste a job description

    Supply the job description, instructions, and any screening questions from an opportunity you found independently.

  4. 04

    Analyse the job requirements

    Separate mandatory requirements from preferred skills and review an explainable alignment summary against your profile.

  5. 05

    Generate tailored application materials

    Use Quick Generate or Guided Generate to draft a tailored CV, cover letter, or screening responses grounded in verified facts.

  6. 06

    Review, edit and download

    Refine sections in the live editor, switch templates, approve the final draft, and download clean PDF or DOCX files.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is CareerCraft AI?

CareerCraft AI is an application preparation workspace that helps you maintain one confirmed career profile and tailor CVs, cover letters, screening answers, and interview practice materials for specific roles you provide.

Is CareerCraft AI a job search platform?

No. CareerCraft AI does not search for jobs, aggregate vacancy listings, connect you with recruiters, or submit applications automatically. You bring job descriptions you have independently found.

Is CareerCraft AI free?

Yes. The initial release is completely free to use, with no paid subscriptions or upgrade walls. Reasonable backend limits apply to daily AI generation requests and file upload sizes to keep the service reliable.

Can I upload my existing CV?

Yes. You can upload PDF or Word (.docx) CVs up to 10 MB. Extracted career details are presented for your review and confirmation before any information is merged into your profile.

Does CareerCraft AI invent experience?

No. CareerCraft AI is designed around strict non-fabrication rules. It only uses confirmed details from your profile, never invents metrics or employers, and highlights missing job requirements so you can clarify them honestly.

Can I download documents in Word and PDF?

Yes. Once you review and approve a generated document, you can export valid, editable Word (.docx) and formatted PDF files generated on the backend.

Can I edit generated documents?

Yes. Every generated document begins as an editable draft. You can update summaries, bullet points, section order, and templates in the Document Editor, and previous versions are saved in version history.

How is my information protected?

Authentication requires verified email sign-in, backend endpoints validate access tokens on every request, database tables enforce Row Level Security per user ID, and uploaded CVs and exported files are stored in private storage buckets.
